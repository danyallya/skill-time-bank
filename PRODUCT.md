# Product brief

Problem: Offer help, request a skill and settle an exchange in agreed hours.

Audience: community organizers and members

## First release

One user role, one core record (exchanges) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Prevent coercion and unsafe requests; define moderation, identity and dispute paths.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
